Timetabs

Reminders for time-blind brains. Mark the tabs that matter and get nudged back to them.

Get started

  1. Pin the extension: click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Timetabs.
  2. On any tab press ⌥⇧P, type 1 and press ↵ to mark it P1.
  3. Add a time to be reminded: 1 30m, 2 fri 9 — anything after that becomes a note.

Check reminders reach you

macOS can silently block Chrome notifications. Send a test — if nothing shows up, switch to the pop-up window, which always works.

Shortcuts

⌥⇧POpen the command bar. Type what you mean, press ↵: 1 30m review CR, fri 9, every 1h, done, snooze
⌥⇧NJump to the most urgent tab
⌥⇧SSnooze everything overdue for 10 minutes
⌥⇧1Mark the selected tabs P1 without opening anything
In the popup↓ into the list, ↵ go, S snooze, D done
