# Privacy policy

Timetabs does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- **What it stores:** which tabs you marked, their priority, notes and reminder times (in Chrome's session storage, cleared when Chrome restarts), and your settings (in Chrome's local extension storage).
- **Where:** only in your browser profile. Timetabs makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.
- **Tab titles and URLs** are read only to show them back to you in the popup and in reminders.

Questions: open an issue on the repository.
